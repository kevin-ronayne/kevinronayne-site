YOUR DOG’S WELL-BEING

Make room for movement.

The domestic dog is a social animal. Companionship, nutritious food, and opportunities for exercise all contribute to a dog’s well-being. With more than 300 breeds recognized by the World Canine Organization, needs vary by age, breed, size, and health.

Diet & exercise

Dogs’ feeding schedules generally change as they mature. Puppies are nursed early in life and gradually transition to regular meals. Work with your veterinarian to choose an appropriate feeding schedule and keep fresh water available.

Every dog is different.

The supplied care guide describes a broad range of 30–120 minutes of daily exercise, depending on breed and size. Ask your veterinarian what is appropriate for your individual dog.

Walks, play sessions, and other safe activities can help support your dog’s health. Activity does not have to be complicated: a brisk walk can be a useful part of the day.

Choose toys that encourage activity

Fetch & retrieval

Age-appropriate balls and retrieval toys can turn outdoor time into engaging movement.

Interactive play

Choose toys that encourage your dog to explore, play, and spend time with you.

Safety first

Choose toys suited to your dog’s age and size, avoid choking hazards, and supervise play when needed.

Grooming & handling

Brush your dog regularly to reduce shedding and help prevent matted fur. Check for ticks and fleas, especially after time in wooded areas. Use pet-formulated soap when bathing, and rinse thoroughly to avoid irritation.

When lifting your dog, support the chest and rear appropriately for their size. Ask your veterinarian for guidance if handling is difficult or painful for your pet.

Vaccinations & medications

Consult your veterinarian about core vaccines and any additional protection recommended for your dog’s environment. The supplied guide discusses protection against rabies, parvovirus, and hepatitis, along with other risks your veterinarian may assess.

General educational information is not a substitute for veterinary care or an individualized feeding or exercise plan.
