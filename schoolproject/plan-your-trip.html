Getting Here

Whether you're arriving by air or sea, your Taniti adventure begins at one of the island's two main arrival points.

Arriving by Air

Most visitors arrive through Taniti's small airport, which currently accommodates small jets and propeller planes.

The airport is being expanded to accommodate larger jets in the coming years.

Visitors who plan to rent a car can find a local rental agency near the airport.

Arriving by Cruise Ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week.

Visitors arriving by cruise ship can explore Taniti City and its nearby beaches. The Merriton Landing area is also easy to explore on foot.

Planning to explore beyond Taniti City? Review the transportation options below before deciding how to travel around the island.

Getting Around

Taniti offers several ways to explore. Your best option will depend on whether you plan to stay near Taniti City or venture into other parts of the island.

  • Public buses: Serve Taniti City daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.
  • Private buses: Serve other areas of the island.
  • Taxis: Available in Taniti City.
  • Rental cars: Available from a local agency near the airport.
  • Bicycles: Available from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.
  • Walking: Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.

Where to Stay

From budget-friendly accommodations to resort experiences, Taniti offers lodging options for a variety of travelers.

🏝️

Four-Star Resort

Taniti has one large four-star resort for visitors interested in a resort-style stay.

🏨

Hotels

The island offers many small, family-owned hotels for travelers seeking traditional accommodations.

🏡

Bed & Breakfasts

A growing number of bed and breakfasts provide additional lodging choices for visitors.

Additional Lodging Options

Travelers seeking inexpensive accommodations can consider Taniti's hostel.

All types of lodging on Taniti are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Accommodation names, current rates, and reservation availability are not included in this tourism prototype.

Food & Dining

Discover local flavors, familiar favorites, and a variety of dining options during your stay on Taniti.

Restaurants

Taniti currently has ten restaurants offering three main types of cuisine.

  • Local Cuisine: Five restaurants primarily serve local fish and rice dishes.
  • American-Style Dining: Three restaurants offer American-style meals.
  • Pan-Asian Cuisine: Two restaurants serve Pan-Asian dishes.

Groceries & Convenience

Visitors who prefer to prepare their own meals or purchase snacks have several grocery options available.

  • Two supermarkets
  • Two smaller grocery stores
  • One convenience store open 24 hours a day

Pubs & Nightlife

Taniti has several pubs, including a microbrewery, as well as a new dance club. Many entertainment activities are located in the developing Merriton Landing area.

Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m. The legal drinking age on Taniti is 18.

Entertainment & Amenities

There's more to Taniti than beaches and sightseeing. Visitors can also enjoy a variety of recreational and cultural activities.

  • Local history museum
  • Art galleries
  • Movie theater
  • Arcade
  • Bowling
  • Pubs and microbrewery
  • Dance club
  • Chartered fishing tours
  • Snorkeling
  • Rainforest zip-lining
  • Helicopter rides

Many of these activities are located in Merriton Landing, a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

A nine-hole golf course is also planned for the island.

Visitor Information

Review these practical details before your visit.

Currency & Payments

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen.

Several banks exchange currency, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English.

English is less common in rural areas, particularly among older residents.

Electrical Outlets

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same voltage used in the United States.

Safety & Healthcare

Violent crime is very rare, but reports of pickpocketing and other petty crime have increased with tourism.

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

National Holidays

Taniti observes many national holidays. Attractions and restaurants may close on these days, so plan accordingly.

Local Rules

Alcohol cannot be served or sold from midnight to 9 a.m.

The legal drinking age is 18. Bicycle helmets are required by law.
